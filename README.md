# Exam Flashcard Prompts

Turn any notes into high-yield flashcards

## Overview
Exam Flashcard Prompts is a digital prompt pack designed for high school and college students drowning in dense study notes. It provides 35 plug-and-play AI prompts that instantly convert your raw lecture notes, textbook chapters, and PDFs into focused, high-yield flashcard decks.

## Use cases
- Pasting 50 pages of biology lecture notes into ChatGPT to generate instant, conceptual flashcards before an exam.
- Breaking down complex legal or historical frameworks into bite-sized active-recall questions when you're too burnt out to write cards manually.
- Organizing your digital flashcard software with built-in spaced repetition metadata without wasting hours formatting tags.

## How to use
1. Download the prompt pack instantly after purchase as a clean PDF and Notion template.
2. Copy any of the 35 subject-agnostic prompts into your AI tool of choice alongside your study notes.
3. Export the generated Q&A pairs directly into your preferred flashcard app and start studying.

## What is included
- 35 subject-agnostic prompts
- Active recall framing
- Spaced repetition tags
